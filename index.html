05How I work

Four habits I bring to every team.

  • 01

    Blameless reviews

    Incident reviews look at systems, not people, and every lesson becomes a lasting improvement.

  • 02

    Automate the manual step

    A step someone has to remember is a step that gets replaced with self-service automation.

  • 03

    Trade-offs on paper

    Options, risks and the reasoning behind a choice are written down before anything gets built.

  • 04

    Async first

    Clear written context by default, so a globally distributed team can move without waiting for a meeting.
